Rework.

A full rebrand for a materials library, from mark to wayfinding.

Brief

A materials library with a decade of stock but a name that no longer described it asked us to rebuild its identity from the ground up. The old mark read as interior decoration; the new one had to read as a working reference — precise enough for architects, warm enough for the public.

Approach

We stripped the identity back to a modular wordmark and a colour system that sorts the collection by material, so a visitor can find their way by hue before they read a word. Printed matter was reset on a strict grid and photography was art-directed to show texture and scale, not lifestyle.

Outcome

The new system launched alongside a reworked entrance and a refreshed archive index. Footfall in the reading room rose in the first quarter, and the library now runs its own identity — with the guidelines and tone of voice we handed over — without us.
