Kestrel.

Product design and identity for a scheduling tool for small teams.

Brief

A two-person startup building a scheduling tool came to us with a working backend and a name, but no product language. They needed an interface their first hundred customers would trust before the feature list was finished.

Approach

We defined a small design system around a single accent and a strict set of spacing rules, then rebuilt the core flow — booking, confirming, rescheduling — around the fewest possible steps. Typography did the heavy lifting so the interface could stay almost entirely black, white and one colour.

Outcome

The redesigned product launched to its first cohort with a clear, consistent interface the team could extend themselves. Churn on the setup flow dropped, and the identity we drew for the launch carried through to their first round of funding materials.
