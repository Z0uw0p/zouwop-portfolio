The room

Worth the trip

A room you can hear yourself in. We cook over oak, pour small-producer wine, and light the room low once the sun drops. Tables sit close enough that strangers trade the last of the bread, and far enough apart that nobody rushes. Come hungry; stay for the last course and the way the candles look on the brick.
